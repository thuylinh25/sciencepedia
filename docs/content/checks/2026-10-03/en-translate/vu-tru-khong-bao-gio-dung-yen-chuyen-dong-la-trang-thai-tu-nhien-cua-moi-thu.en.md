## 🌍 You are moving faster than you think

Right now, as you read this article, your body is taking part in several different motions at once.

### Earth is spinning

Earth turns once on its axis every day.

At the equator, this speed reaches about:

> **1,670 km/h**

Even though you cannot feel it, you are turning with the planet at roughly the speed of a jet airliner.

### Earth orbits the Sun

As well as spinning, Earth races around the Sun at about:

> **107,000 km/h (29.8 km/s)**

In a single second, we travel almost 30 km through space.

### The Solar System orbits the Milky Way

The Sun and the whole Solar System are not standing still either.

They are orbiting the centre of the Milky Way at about:

> **790,000 km/h (220 km/s)**

It takes about 230 million years to complete one orbit around the Galaxy.

### The Milky Way is moving too

The Milky Way itself is also hurtling through intergalactic space at millions of km/h under the gravitational pull of the surrounding galaxies and galaxy clusters.

No level of the universe is truly standing still.

---

## 🌌 Not only matter moves — space does too

Stranger still, the motion of the universe does not come only from objects flying through space.

**Space itself is expanding.**

In 1929, the astronomer **Edwin Hubble** discovered that distant galaxies are moving away from us.

This discovery showed that:

> The universe is not a static box with matter inside it.

Instead, the "box" itself is growing over time.

You can picture the universe as a balloon being inflated:

- Galaxies do not necessarily have to fly away from one another on their own.
- The distance between them grows because the space in between is stretching.

---

## ⚡ The expansion is speeding up

For most of the 20th century, scientists thought gravity would slow the expansion down.

But in the late 1990s, observations of distant [[sieu-tan-tinh|supernovae]] showed the opposite:

> The universe is expanding faster and faster.

To explain this, physicists introduced the concept of:

> **[[nang-luong-toi|Dark energy]]**

Dark energy is currently thought to make up about:

> **68% of the total energy of the universe**

and to be the main driver of the accelerating expansion.

---

## 🧪 Motion exists even in the microscopic world

It is not only planets and galaxies that move.

At the atomic level, matter is also always in motion.

Atoms:

- Vibrate constantly.
- Collide constantly.
- Exchange energy constantly.

The temperature of an object is really a measure of the average motion of the particles inside it.

Even in extremely cold environments, quantum effects mean that matter is never completely motionless.

---

## ⏳ Motion makes time and life

Every process in nature depends on change.

- Planets orbit stars.
- Atoms form chemical bonds.
- [[DNA]] replicates.
- Cells divide.
- The brain transmits nerve signals.

All of these are forms of motion.

Without motion:

- There would be no chemical reactions.
- No biological evolution.
- No weather.
- No life.
- No concept of time as we experience it.

In other words:

> Motion does not just happen in the universe; it is what makes the universe alive.

---

## 🌠 Is there such a thing as absolute rest?

According to modern physics, there is no absolute frame of reference for deciding whether an object is "truly at rest".

An object can be:

- At rest relative to the ground.
- But moving relative to the Sun.
- Moving relative to the Milky Way.
- Moving relative to the cosmic microwave background.

"At rest" is always a relative concept.

---

## 📌 Conclusion

The universe is not a static stage with objects moving across it. The stage itself is changing and expanding every second.

🌌 Earth spins on its axis.

☀️ Earth orbits the Sun.

🌌 The Solar System orbits the Milky Way.

🌀 The Milky Way hurtles through space.

✨ And space itself is expanding.

Change is not the exception in the universe. Change is its most natural state
